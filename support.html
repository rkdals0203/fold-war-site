Fold War Support

A game for two people sitting side by side with one phone on its side. Fold the paper so the ink dot you drop lands on the mirror spot in the other camp, and knock down all the enemy soldiers first.

FAQ

How do I play?
Your first match is the tutorial. Drag your soldiers into place and press Ready. On your turn, tap your side to drop a dot, then drag your paper's edge over the fold.
Can I see the tutorial again?
Yes, turn it back on in Settings.
Can my soldiers be placed at random?
While placing, tap the dice to shuffle, or hold it to have them shuffled when the match starts (this round only, and only for you).
How do I turn off sound or haptics?
In Settings. With the silent switch on, the game makes no sound.
Does it need the internet? Does it collect data?
No. It works offline and collects nothing. Privacy Policy
