Fold War Privacy Policy

Effective: November 11, 2026

Fold War ("the app") is an offline game for two people sharing one phone. The app does not collect or transmit any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for or collect names, email addresses, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or any other personal information. There is no account or sign-in.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps only these settings on your device. They never leave it, and they are removed when you delete the app.

  • Sound and haptics on/off
  • The soldier count you last chose
  • Whether the tutorial is done, and how often a tip has been shown

Network, advertising and tracking

The app does not connect to the internet and uses no advertising, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs. It does not track your activity across other apps or websites.

Children

Because the app collects no information, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
